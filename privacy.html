What we collect

When you place an order, our payment processor, Stripe, collects your name, email, shipping address and payment details to process your payment and ship your order. We never see or store your full card number.

If you contact us or sign up for our emails, we keep your name, email and message so we can reply.

Your shopping cart is saved only in your own browser until you check out.

How we use it

To fulfill and ship orders, answer your questions, and — only if you signed up — send you news about new arrivals. You can unsubscribe at any time.

Sharing

We share information only with the services we need to run the store: Stripe (payments), Netlify (website hosting and forms) and our shipping carrier. We do not sell your information.
